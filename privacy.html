Privacy notice

A small website with a deliberately small data footprint.

Wisebrook has no user accounts, contact form, comment system, booking engine, analytics package or advertising tracker.

Last updated: 16 August 2026

At a glance

What the site does—and does not—collect.

Accounts & forms

None

There is no sign-in, profile, newsletter field, reservation form or contact form on wisebrook.org.

Analytics & advertising

None

The current site does not load an analytics service, behavioural advertising pixel or cross-site tracking script.

Site cookies

None in the site code

Wisebrook does not set a preference, marketing or analytics cookie. Hosting security measures are described below.

The detail

Some technical processing occurs whenever a browser requests a page from a web host.

1. Cloudflare hosting

Wisebrook is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. When a browser requests a page, Cloudflare processes technical request information needed to deliver and protect the service. That information can include an IP address, request time, requested address, browser or user-agent information, referring address and security events.

Cloudflare handles this information under its own privacy and security practices. Wisebrook does not use those technical logs to build visitor profiles or serve targeted advertising. Cloudflare may apply necessary security technologies when protecting the service; the Wisebrook site code itself does not set cookies.

2. Email

The contact page uses ordinary email links rather than a web form. If you send an email to contact@wisebrook.org or support@wisebrook.org, your email address, message, attachments and standard delivery metadata are processed by your email provider and the provider used for the Wisebrook mailbox.

Messages are used to review the request, reply where appropriate, document a correction or address abuse and security issues. Do not send payment-card data, identification documents, account credentials, medical records or other information that is not necessary for the request.

3. External links

Wisebrook links to hotels, casinos, public authorities, support services and photography pages. Following one of those links takes you to a separately operated website with its own privacy practices. Wisebrook does not receive reservation or gambling-account data from those websites.

4. Children and age-restricted subject matter

The guide discusses casino areas intended for adults aged 18+ or 19+, depending on the province. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly request personal information from them. The website does not provide gambling or an age-verification account.

5. Requests about an email

If you have sent Wisebrook an email and want to ask about, correct or delete the personal information in that correspondence, write to contact@wisebrook.org from the same address where practical. A request may need enough information to identify the relevant message, and some information may be retained where reasonably required for security, legal compliance or an ongoing dispute.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect the site and editorial mailbox. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Changes

This notice will be revised if the site adds a feature that changes its data practices. The updated date at the top of the page identifies the current version.

8. Questions

Privacy questions may be sent to contact@wisebrook.org. Technical and security reports may be sent to support@wisebrook.org.
