Scope

Names are reference points, not recommendations.

The register runs west to east within each province and carries no score, preference, or commercial placement.

Property facts are attributed to official hotel, casino, or public-sector sources. Wisebrook does not reproduce live prices, hours, availability, or reservation information.

Casino age limits are provincial: 18+ in Alberta and Québec, 19+ in British Columbia. Venue-specific identification and access policies still apply.

Why Kelowna replaced Penticton

The original reference treated Penticton Lakeside Resort as a casino hotel. BCLC records show that casino closed there in April 2017; Cascades Casino Penticton now operates at a separate address. Wisebrook therefore uses a current co-located Okanagan example.
