Responsible gambling

The casino floor can remain an optional part of the stay.

Wisebrook does not provide gambling, accounts, deposits or rewards. This page puts age rules, practical boundaries and direct support links beside the travel information.

Entry rules

Legal casino age depends on the province.

Alberta

18 or older

Casino gaming areas are restricted to adults aged 18+. The hotel and other public areas may operate under different access rules.

British Columbia

19 or older

Provincial gaming areas are restricted to adults aged 19+. Current law requires the age restriction to be displayed at public entrances to gaming areas.

Québec

18 or older

Loto-Québec casinos restrict entry to visitors aged 18+. Anyone who appears younger may be asked for government-issued photo identification.

Bring valid government-issued identification and follow the rules posted by the property. Entry requirements and access routes can change.

Before entering

A limit works best when it is decided before play begins, not while the outcome is changing.

Make the boundary part of the itinerary

  • Choose whether to enter at all. A casino is not required to make use of the hotel, landscape, restaurant or wellness facilities.
  • Set a firm spending limit. Use only discretionary money that is not needed for travel, housing, food, debt or savings.
  • Set an end time. Plan what comes next—a meal, walk or return to the room—so the session has a clear edge.
  • Do not chase a loss. Every outcome remains uncertain. A previous loss does not make a future win more likely.
  • Avoid borrowing and limit access to funds. Leave extra cards behind if that makes the boundary easier to keep.
  • Step away if judgment is affected. Fatigue, alcohol, cannabis, distress and urgency can all make a planned limit harder to follow.

Canada’s Lower-Risk Gambling Guidelines provide evidence-informed limits for adults who choose to gamble. Lower risk does not mean no risk, and choosing not to gamble is always a valid option.

A useful signal

Concern is enough reason to pause. A person does not need to wait for a crisis to ask for help.

When play no longer feels contained

Consider contacting a support service if gambling is being hidden, financed with borrowed money, used to escape stress, continued beyond planned limits, or causing conflict, debt, anxiety or lost time. Provincial services can also support family members and friends.

This page is general information, not medical advice. If anyone is in immediate danger, call emergency services.
